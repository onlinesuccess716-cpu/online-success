Article: Freelancing Kya Hai?

Freelancing ka matlab hai ke aap kisi aik company ke mulazim nahi hote, balkay apni maharat se mukhtalif logon ke liye kaam karte hain. Aap ghar baithe likhai, design, data entry ya video ka kaam kar sakte hain.

Shuru mein kabhi bhi kisi ko advance paise na dein. Hamesha mashhoor platform se kaam len aur apni profile achi banayen.

Aksar Puche Jane Wale Sawalat

Freelancing ke liye kitne paise chahiye?

Kuch bhi nahi. Aap ko sirf mobile ya laptop aur internet chahiye. Skill aap YouTube se free mein seekh sakte hain.

Kya mobile se freelancing ho sakti hai?

Ji haan, shuru mein video editing, social media management aur data entry mobile se ho sakti hai. Lekin behtar kaam ke liye laptop behtar hai.

Freelancing mein fraud se kaise bachen?

Kisi ko advance paise na den. Hamesha Fiverr ya Upwork jaisay mashhoor platform se kaam len. Jo kahe 'pehle fee do phir kaam mile ga' wo fraud hai.

Pehla order kitne din mein aayega?

Aam tor par 1 se 2 mahine lag sakte hain. Agar aap roz mehnat karen, Gig achi banayen aur jaldi jawab den to order jaldi aa sakta hai.

Aakhri Baat: Freelancing koi jadoo nahi hai ke aaj shuru karen aur kal lakh pati ban jayen. Lekin agar aap 3 mahine lagatar mehnat karen ge to aap ki kamai shuru ho jaye gi. Aaj hi ek skill chunen aur seekhna shuru kar den.

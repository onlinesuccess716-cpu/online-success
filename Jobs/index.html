Zaroori Article

Article: Nokri Ka Ishtehar Kaise Samjhen?

Jab bhi koi nokri ka ishtehar dekhen to sab se pehle taleem, tajurba, umar ki had aur aakhri tareekh zaroor parhen. Agar koi nokri paise mange to samajh jayen woh jali hai. Sarkari nokri ke liye hamesha sarkari website se maloomat len.

Apni CV hamesha saaf suthri aur sach par mabni banayen.

Ishtehar Mein Kya Kya Check Karen?

Darkhwast Dene Se Pehle Ye 6 Chezen Zaroor Dekhen

  • Taleem: Matric, FA, BA ya Master - kya manga hai?
  • Tajurba: Kitne saal ka tajurba chahiye?
  • Umar Ki Had: Kam se kam aur zyada se zyada umar kitni hai?
  • Aakhri Tareekh: Darkhwast ki last date kab hai?
  • Tankhwah Aur Jagah: Salary kitni hai aur job kahan hai?
  • Darkhwast Ka Tareeqa: Online hai ya Dak ke zariye?

Jali Nokri Se Kaise Bachen?

Agar Ye 3 Nishaniyan Hon To Samajh Jayen - Ye Jali Hai

  • Paise Mange: 'Registration Fee', 'Training Fee' ya 'File Fee' mange to 100% jali hai.
  • Bina Interview Ke Nokri: Jo kahe 'aap select ho gaye, bas paise bhej den' wo jhooth hai.
  • Sarkari Website Par Na Ho: Sarkari nokri hamesha sarkari website par hoti hai, sirf WhatsApp par nahi.
Mashwara: Kabhi bhi kisi ko apna CNIC ki copy, bank account ya OTP WhatsApp par na bhejen. Sarkari nokri ke liye kabhi koi agent paise nahi leta.

Aksar Puche Jane Wale Sawalat

Kya sarkari nokri ke liye paise dene parte hain?

Nahi, bilkul nahi. Sirf sarkari challan / fee hoti hai jo bank mein jama hoti hai, kisi bande ko haath mein nahi deni. Jo paise mange wo fraud hai.

CV mein kya kya likhna chahiye?

Naam, rabta number, taleem, tajurba aur hunar. Jhooth kabhi na likhen. CV 1 se 2 safhon se zyada lambi na ho.

WhatsApp par aayi nokri sahih hai ya jali?

Zyada tar jali hoti hai. Pehle us nokri ka naam Google par ya sarkari website par check karen. Agar wahan na mile to darkhwast na den.

Matric ke baad kaun si nokri mil sakti hai?

Police, Army, clerk, driver, data entry aur private company mein kaam mil sakta hai. Sath sath taleem bhi jari rakhen taake aage behtar nokri mile.

Aakhri Baat: Nokri ke liye kabhi jaldi na karen aur kabhi paise de kar nokri na len. Pehle ishtehar ko achi tarah parhen, sarkari website se tasdeeq karen, phir darkhwast den. Mehnat aur sabar se hi achi nokri milti hai.

Article: Online Dhoke Se Kaise Bachen?

Internet par kabhi bhi apna OTP, password ya bank ki tafseel kisi ko na batayen. Agar koi kahe aap ne inaam jeeta hai ya aap ka account band ho raha hai to woh aksar jhoot hota hai. Mazboot password rakhen aur har app ka password alag rakhen.

Mehfooz rehna hi asal kamyabi hai.

Aam Online Dhoke Kaise Hote Hain?

Ye 5 Dhoke Sab Se Zyada Hote Hain

  • Inaam Wala Dhoka: 'Aap ne 50,000 jeeta hai, bas fee bhej den' - ye 100% jhoot hai.
  • Bank Wala Dhoka: 'Main bank se bol raha hoon, OTP batayen' - bank kabhi OTP nahi mangta.
  • Account Band Wala Dhoka: 'Aap ka account band ho raha hai, link par click karen' - ye nakli link hota hai.
  • Nokri Wala Dhoka: 'Nokri pakki hai, bas paise bhej den' - asli nokri kabhi paise nahi mangti.
  • Sasta Saman Wala Dhoka: 'Bohat sasta iPhone, pehle paise bhej den' - paise bhej kar cheez nahi aati.

Mazboot Password Kaise Banayen?

Mazboot Password Ke 4 Usool

  • Lamba Rakhen: Kam se kam 8 se 12 harf ka ho.
  • Mila Kar Banayen: Harf + Number + Nishan, jaise Meri@Dukan123
  • Har App Ka Alag: Facebook, Gmail aur Bank ka password ek jaisa na rakhen.
  • Kisi Ko Na Batayen: Apna password dost ya rishtedar ko bhi na batayen.
Mashwara: Agar koi aap ko daraye ke 'account band ho jayega' to ghabrayen nahi. Pehle us bank / company ki asli website ya asli number par khud rabta karen, usi message wale number par jawab na den.

Aksar Puche Jane Wale Sawalat

Kya bank kabhi OTP mangta hai?

Nahi, kabhi nahi. Bank, JazzCash ya Easypaisa wala kabhi call ya WhatsApp par OTP nahi mangta. Jo mange wo chor hai.

Agar ghalti se OTP bata diya to kya karen?

Foran apna password badlen, bank ko call karen aur account ko block karwayen. Der na karen, warna paise nikal sakte hain.

Kya free WiFi mehfooz hai?

Nahi, free WiFi par bank app ya Gmail na kholen. Wahan se aap ki maloomat chori ho sakti hai. Zaroori kaam apne mobile data par karen.

Nakli link ko kaise pehchanen?

Nakli link mein naam mein ghalti hoti hai, jaise faceb0ok ya bank-pk-free. Asli link hamesha asli website jaisa hota hai. Shak ho to link par click na karen.

Aakhri Baat: Internet par sab se bari hifazat yehi hai ke apna OTP, password aur bank ki tafseel kisi ko na batayen. Jo cheez muft ya bohat sasti lage, ya jo aap ko daraye, us par foran yaqeen na karen. Pehle tasdeeq karen, phir faisla karen.

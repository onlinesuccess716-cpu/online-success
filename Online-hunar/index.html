Article: Online Hunar Kya Hai?

Aaj ke dor mein online hunar seekhna bohat zaroori ho gaya hai. Aap mobile ya computer se typing, graphic design, video editing, aur internet ka sahi istemal seekh sakte hain. Shuru mein aik hi hunar par tawajja dein, rozana thora waqt dein aur practice karte rahen.

Yaad rakhen, kamyabi aik din mein nahi milti. Musalsal mehnat hi aap ko aage le kar jaye gi.

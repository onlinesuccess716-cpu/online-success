Zaroori

Hum Kya Maloomat Lete Hain?

Jab aap hamari website kholte hain to hum aap ka naam, password ya bank ki tafseel nahi mangte. Sirf ye aam maloomat ho sakti hai: aap ne kaun sa page dekha, aap kis shehar ya mulk se aaye, aur aap ne mobile ya computer istemal kiya. Ye maloomat sirf website ko behtar banane ke liye hoti hai.

Cookies Kya Hain?

Cookies Ka Asaan Matlab

  • Cookies chhoti files hoti hain jo website ko yaad rakhti hain ke aap ne pehle kya dekha.
  • Hum cookies se aap ka password ya bank ki tafseel nahi lete.
  • Aap chahein to apne browser se cookies band kar sakte hain, website phir bhi chalegi.

Teesri Company - Ads Aur Links

Google Ads Aur Bahar Ke Links

  • Hamari website par Google ke ads aa sakte hain. Google apni policy ke mutabiq ads dikhata hai.
  • Hamari website se bahar ke links par click karne par un ki apni privacy policy hogi, hamari nahi.
  • Hum aap ki maloomat kisi ko bechte nahi.
Mashwara: Koi bhi website aap se OTP, password ya bank PIN mange to us ko kabhi na den.
